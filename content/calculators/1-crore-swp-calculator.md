## The Mathematics of Capital Longevity: Withdrawal Rate vs. Compounding Velocity

The longevity of a retirement corpus is governed by the dynamic interplay between your **Monthly Withdrawal Rate ($W$)**, your **Portfolio Growth Rate ($r$)**, and your **Annual Inflation Hike ($s$)**:

$$ B_{m} = B_{m-1} \times (1 + r_m) - W_m $$

Where:
- **$B_m$** = Portfolio balance at the end of month $m$
- **$r_m$** = Monthly portfolio compounding return ($\text{Annual Return} \div 12$)
- **$W_m$** = Monthly cash payout withdrawn at month $m$

### The Golden Rule of Perpetual SWP
If your annualized withdrawal rate is equal to or less than your portfolio's net compounding return ($W_{\text{annual}} \le r$), **your principal corpus never depletes**. At an 8% expected portfolio return on ₹1 Crore:
- $8\% \times \text{₹1,00,00,000} = \text{₹8,00,000 / year} = \text{₹66,667 / month}$.
- Withdrawing ₹60,000 to ₹66,667 per month allows you to withdraw cash indefinitely without touching the original ₹1 Crore principal!

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Monthly Payout** | ₹58,333 / month | **₹60,000 / month** |

> **Bottom Line:** A Mutual Fund SWP delivers **₹16,000+ extra in-hand cash every single month** compared to a bank FD on the exact same ₹1 Crore starting corpus due to capital gains tax arbitrage.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I withdraw ₹1 Lakh per month from a ₹1 Crore corpus?
Withdrawing ₹1,00,000 per month represents a 12% annual withdrawal rate. Even at an 8% expected return, withdrawing ₹1 Lakh/month will deplete a ₹1 Crore corpus in **approximately 14 years**. To sustainably withdraw ₹1 Lakh/month for 25+ years, an investor requires a starting corpus of **₹1.60 to ₹2.00 Crores**.

### Which mutual funds are best for an SWP from ₹1 Crore?
For a sustainable post-retirement SWP, advisors recommend a **Conservative Hybrid Fund**, **Equity Savings Fund**, or **Balanced Advantage Fund (BAF)**. These funds maintain 35%–65% equity for growth while hedging the remainder in debt and arbitrage instruments to eliminate sequence-of-returns risk.

### Is the capital gains tax deducted automatically during SWP?
No. Indian mutual fund houses do not deduct TDS on SWP redemptions for resident Indian investors. You receive the full gross withdrawal in your bank account on your chosen payout date (e.g., 5th or 10th of every month). Your capital gains statement is issued at the end of the fiscal year for filing your ITR.
