## 4. Taxation Under Budget 2024 (Section 112A)

Mutual fund redemptions are governed by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024:

- **Holding Period $\gt$ 12 Months (LTCG):** Taxed at **12.5% base rate (13.0% effective with 4% cess)** on net aggregate long-term capital gains exceeding the annual statutory limit of **₹1,25,000**.
- **Holding Period $\le$ 12 Months (STCG):** Taxed at a flat **20.0% (effective 20.8% with cess)**.
- **Tax Harvesting Strategy:** For a ₹1 Lakh SIP investor, redeeming units worth ₹1.25 Lakh of profit each financial year and immediately reinvesting resets your cost basis, saving up to ₹16,250 annually in taxes.
- **Retirement Transition:** When transitioning from ₹1 Lakh SIP accumulation to retirement cash flows, using an [SWP Calculator](/swp-calculator) or [1 Crore SWP Calculator](/1-crore-swp-calculator) allows you to withdraw systematic income where only the capital gain portion is taxed, preserving up to 50%+ more post-tax cash compared to Bank Fixed Deposits.
