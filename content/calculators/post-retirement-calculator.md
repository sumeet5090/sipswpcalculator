### The 3-Bucket Asset Allocation Strategy

To safeguard against **Sequence of Returns Risk (SRR)**—the danger of suffering a market crash early in retirement—our engine dynamically organizes your investable nest egg into three disciplined operational buckets:

1. **Bucket 1: Immediate Cash Buffer (0 to 3 Years):**
   - **Capital Allocation:** Exactly 3 years of net monthly SWP drawdowns ($\min(\text{Corpus}, 36 \times W_1)$).
   - **Instruments:** Liquid Mutual Funds, Ultra Short-Duration Funds, and Bank Fixed Deposits.
   - **Purpose:** Shields your immediate living expenses from equity market volatility. You never sell equities during a market downturn to buy groceries.

2. **Bucket 2: Fixed Income & Capital Preservation (4 to 10 Years):**
   - **Capital Allocation:** Up to 40% of the remaining investable corpus.
   - **Instruments:** Conservative Hybrid Mutual Funds, Corporate Bond Funds, Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS), and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs).
   - **Purpose:** Delivers steady, predictable yields that regularly replenish Bucket 1 as living expenses are disbursed.

3. **Bucket 3: Long-Term Inflation-Beating Growth (10+ Years):**
   - **Capital Allocation:** The remaining corpus balance.
   - **Instruments:** Flexi-Cap Equity Funds, Large-Cap Index Funds (Nifty 50), and Multi-Asset Allocation Funds.
   - **Purpose:** Ensures the portfolio compounds ahead of 6% to 7% healthcare and lifestyle inflation over a 25-to-30-year horizon.

### Dynamic Deficit-Only SWP Modeling

A common mistake in retirement planning is calculating systematic withdrawals against total household expenditure without offsetting guaranteed pensions. In our model:

$$\text{Net Monthly SWP } (W_m) = \max(0, E_m - P_{\text{guaranteed}})$$

Where $E_m$ is your monthly living expense (escalating annually by inflation $g$) and $P_{\text{guaranteed}}$ is your fixed monthly pension from NPS annuity or employer pension. Because the pension remains steady, your net portfolio drawdown increases over time only to bridge the inflation-adjusted gap, significantly extending your portfolio longevity compared to traditional flat withdrawal models.

### Key Indian Statutory Tax Protections at Superannuation

- **Statutory Gratuity Exemption:** Covered employees under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 enjoy 100% tax exemption up to ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakhs) under Section 10(10).
- **NPS Tier-1 Lump Sum Exemption:** Under Section 10(12A), the 60% lump-sum exit payout is completely exempt from income tax.
- **Mutual Fund SWP Taxation (Section 112A):** Under the Finance (No. 2) Act 2024, mutual fund SWP redemptions are subject to Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax at 12.5% only on the gains component exceeding the annual ₹1.25 Lakh exemption limit. Because early SWP installments consist predominantly of principal return, your effective tax rate during early retirement years remains exceptionally low.
