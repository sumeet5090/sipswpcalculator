## 5. Tax Impact Under Budget 2024 Section 112A Rules

Mutual fund redemptions are governed by the statutory capital gains framework updated under the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024:

- **Holding Period:** Greater than 12 months qualifies as Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG).
- **LTCG Tax Rate:** Flat **12.5% base rate (13.0% effective with 4% cess)** on cumulative net gains exceeding **₹1.25 Lakh per financial year**.
- **Tax Harvesting Strategy:** Annually redeeming and immediately reinvesting up to ₹1.25 Lakh of long-term gains utilizes your statutory exemption limit, resetting your cost basis and saving up to ₹16,250 in taxes each financial year.
- **Retirement Decumulation:** Upon retirement, transition your corpus into a [Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)](/swp-calculator) or [50 Lakh SWP](/50-lakh-swp-calculator) to draw tax-advantaged monthly cash flows.
