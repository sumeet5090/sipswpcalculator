## What is Inflation and Why Does It Matter?

**Inflation** is the steady, continuous increase in the prices of goods and services over time, which directly erodes the **purchasing power** of your money. In simple terms, a ₹100 note today will buy significantly fewer groceries, healthcare services, or fuel 10 years from now.

For Indian investors, inflation is the silent destroyer of wealth. If your savings sit in traditional fixed deposits yielding $6.5\%$ while consumer price inflation runs at $6.0\%$, your **real post-tax rate of return is negative**. To build lasting wealth, your investment portfolio must consistently outpace inflation.

### The Inflation Mathematical Equations

1. **Future Cost Escalation (Forward Inflation):**
   $$\mathbf{FV = PV \cdot (1 + i)^t}$$
   *   **$FV$** = Estimated future cost of a current expense
   *   **$PV$** = Present cost of the item or monthly budget
   *   **$i$** = Annual inflation rate in decimal (e.g. $6\% = 0.06$)
   *   **$t$** = Number of years into the future

2. **Purchasing Power Parity (Reverse Discounting):**
   $$\mathbf{PV = \frac{FV}{(1 + i)^t}}$$
   *   Shows what a future lump sum (e.g., a ₹1 Crore retirement corpus) is actually worth in today's money.
