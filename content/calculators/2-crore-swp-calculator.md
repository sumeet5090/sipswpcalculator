## The Mathematics of Capital Longevity: Withdrawal Rate vs. Compounding Velocity

The survival of a retirement corpus depends on the balance between your **Monthly Withdrawal Rate ($W$)**, your **Portfolio Growth Rate ($r$)**, and your **Annual Inflation Hike ($s$)**:

$$ B_{m} = B_{m-1} \times (1 + r_m) - W_m $$

Where:
- **$B_m$** = Portfolio balance at the end of month $m$
- **$r_m$** = Monthly portfolio compounding return ($\text{Annual Return} \div 12$)
- **$W_m$** = Monthly cash payout withdrawn at month $m$

### The Golden Rule of Perpetual SWP
If your annualized withdrawal rate is equal to or less than your portfolio's net compounding return ($W_{\text{annual}} \le r$), **your principal corpus never depletes**. At an 8% expected portfolio return on ₹2 Crores:
- $8\% \times \text{₹2,00,00,000} = \text{₹16,00,000 / year} = \text{₹1,33,333 / month}$.
- Withdrawing ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,33,333 per month allows you to withdraw cash indefinitely without touching the original ₹2 Crore principal!
