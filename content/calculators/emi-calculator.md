## What is an Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)?

An **Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)** is a fixed monthly payment made by a borrower to a lender (bank or NBFC) on a specified date of every calendar month. EMIs are structured to pay off both the loan **interest** and **principal** over a designated tenure, ensuring that at the end of the term, the loan balance reaches exactly zero.

In the initial years of a long-tenure loan (such as a 20-year Home Loan), the bulk of each monthly EMI goes toward servicing the interest burden. As the outstanding balance gradually reduces, the principal repayment component accelerates.

### The Standard Loan EMI Formula

Financial institutions in India calculate monthly EMI using the reducing-balance amortization equation:

$$\mathbf{E = P \cdot r \cdot \frac{(1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}}$$

*   **$E$** = Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)
*   **$P$** = Principal loan amount borrowed
*   **$r$** = Monthly interest rate ($r = \frac{\text{Annual Interest Rate}}{12 \times 100}$)
*   **$n$** = Loan tenure in number of months ($n = \text{Years} \times 12$)

> **Crucial Financial Takeaway:** On a 25-year loan, you pay **more in interest (₹42.47 Lakh) than the original amount you borrowed (₹30 Lakh)**. Prepaying even one extra EMI per year or increasing your payment by 5% annually can shave 6 to 8 years off your loan tenure and save over ₹15 Lakh in interest.
