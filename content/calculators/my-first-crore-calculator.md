Reaching your **first ₹1 Crore** is widely considered the single most transformative milestone in an investor's wealth creation journey. Thanks to the power of compounding, getting from ₹0 to ₹1 Crore takes the longest, while getting from ₹1 Crore to ₹2 Crore often takes less than half the time.

This **My First Crore Calculator** is designed as a **Goal-Seek Tool**. Instead of guessing an investment amount and seeing what it grows to, you start with your final goal—**₹1 Crore (or any custom corpus)**—and reverse-engineer the exact monthly Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) required to reach it.

## How to Use the Target Goal Calculator

1. **Target Goal Amount (Corpus):** Set your desired target (defaulted to ₹1,00,00,000 / 1 Crore).
2. **Time to Goal (Years):** Choose your investment horizon (e.g., 5, 10, 15, or 20 years).
3. **Expected Return Rate (p.a):** Enter the expected annualized growth rate of your mutual fund portfolio (e.g., 12% for equity mutual funds).

The interactive calculator immediately calculates the exact monthly SIP required and displays a dynamic chart breaking down your total contributions versus the capital gains earned.

## How Much Monthly SIP is Required to Build ₹1 Crore?

Assuming an average **12% annual return** from equity mutual funds, here is a quick breakdown of the monthly SIP needed across different timeframes:

| Time Horizon | Target Corpus | Expected Return | Monthly SIP Required | Total Investment | Wealth Gained |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5 Years** | ₹1 Crore | 12% p.a. | **₹1,22,444** | ₹73,46,664 | ₹26,53,336 |
| **10 Years** | ₹1 Crore | 12% p.a. | **₹43,041** | ₹51,64,867 | ₹48,35,133 |

> **Key Takeaway:** Extending your investment horizon from 10 years to 20 years reduces your required monthly investment by over **75%** (from ~₹43,000/mo to ~₹10,000/mo), proving that time in the market is vastly more powerful than timing the market.

## The 15x15x15 Rule for Making ₹1 Crore

A popular rule of thumb in Indian personal finance is the **15x15x15 Rule**:
- Invest **₹15,000** per month
- For **15 Years**
- At an expected return of **15% p.a.**

Doing this results in a total accumulated corpus of **₹1.01 Crore** on an investment of just ₹27 Lakhs!

## Accelerate Your Journey with Step-Up SIPs

If starting with ₹43,000/month for a 10-year goal feels daunting, you can use a **Step-Up (Top-Up) SIP strategy**. By increasing your SIP amount by just **10% every year** as your salary grows, you can start with a much smaller initial monthly contribution and still reach ₹1 Crore in the exact same time frame.
