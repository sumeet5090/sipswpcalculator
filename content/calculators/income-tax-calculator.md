### Section 10(13A) HRA Exemption Formula (Old Regime)

If you live in rented accommodation and opt for the **Old Tax Regime**, your tax-exempt House Rent Allowance (HRA) is the **minimum** of three statutory thresholds:

1. **Actual HRA Received** from your employer.
2. **Excess Rent Over 10% of Basic + DA:** $\max(0, \text{Annual Rent Paid} - 0.10 \times \text{Basic + DA})$.
3. **City Classification Ceiling:** **50% of Basic + DA** for Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai) or **40% of Basic + DA** for Non-Metro cities (including Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad).
