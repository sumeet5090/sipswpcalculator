Every major life goal—whether it is buying your dream home, funding your child's higher education, or building a comfortable retirement nest egg—starts with a **Target Corpus**. 

Traditional SIP calculators ask for an investment amount and tell you the final corpus. The **Target Corpus Calculator** does the exact opposite: you specify your desired financial milestone (e.g. ₹50 Lakhs, ₹1 Crore, or ₹5 Crores), your timeline, and expected rate of return, and it reverse-engineers the **exact monthly SIP** required to reach that target with mathematical precision.

## How Does the Target Corpus Calculator Work?

Under the hood, the calculator solves the inverse annuity-due compounding equation:

$$ \text{Required SIP} = \frac{\text{Target Corpus} \times r}{(1 + r) \times \left[ (1 + r)^n - 1 \right]} $$

Where:
- **Target Corpus**: Desired accumulated wealth at maturity.
- **$r$**: Monthly rate of return ($\text{Annual Rate} / 12 / 100$).
- **$n$**: Total number of months ($\text{Years} \times 12$).

If you enable an **Annual Step-Up (Top-Up)**, the calculator uses an iterative binary search engine to find the exact starting monthly SIP that scales with your annual salary appraisals.
