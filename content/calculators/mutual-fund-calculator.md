## Why Use a Mutual Fund Calculator?

Investing without a plan is like driving blind. A mutual fund calculator helps you:
- **Set Realistic Goals:** Understand exactly how much you need to invest monthly to reach ₹1 Crore or your retirement target.
- **Account for Inflation:** By projecting future values, you can adjust your strategy using step-up SIPs to beat inflation.
- **Understand Taxation:** Factor in Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) and Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) to see your true post-tax returns.

## How Mutual Fund Returns Work

Mutual funds generate returns through capital appreciation and dividends. Over long periods, equity mutual funds in India have historically delivered 12-15% CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate). However, returns are not guaranteed and fluctuate with market conditions.

Use these tools to model conservative (10%), moderate (12%), and aggressive (15%) scenarios.
