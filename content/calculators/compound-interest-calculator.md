## What is Compound Interest?

**Compound Interest** is the mathematical engine behind long-term wealth creation. Unlike Simple Interest—which only earns returns on the initial principal—compound interest earns **interest on previously earned interest**. Over extended periods, this compounding acceleration turns modest savings into exponential wealth.

Albert Einstein famously referred to compound interest as the *"eighth wonder of the world: he who understands it, earns it; he who doesn't, pays it."*

### The Compound Interest Formula

The future value of an investment compounded periodically is determined by:

$$\mathbf{A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}}$$

*   **A** = Final accumulated amount (Principal + Total Interest)
*   **P** = Initial principal investment (deposit)
*   **r** = Annual nominal interest rate (in decimal format, e.g., $12\% = 0.12$)
*   **n** = Compounding frequency per year ($1$ for Annual, $2$ for Half-Yearly, $4$ for Quarterly, $12$ for Monthly)
*   **t** = Total investment horizon in years

## Compounding Frequencies: Why Frequency Matters

The frequency with which interest is credited directly impacts the **Effective Annual Rate (EAR)**:

1. **Annual Compounding ($n = 1$):** Interest is calculated and added once at the end of every year. Common in instruments like public provident funds.
2. **Quarterly Compounding ($n = 4$):** Standard banking practice in India for Bank Fixed Deposits (FDs) and National Savings Certificates (NSC).
3. **Monthly Compounding ($n = 12$):** Common in recurring deposits and corporate fixed deposits.
4. **Continuous / Daily Compounding ($n = 365$):** Used in mutual fund NAV calculations, where portfolio earnings compound every business day.

### The Rule of 72: Quick Doubling Estimate

The **Rule of 72** is a mental shortcut to estimate how many years it takes for your investment to double at a fixed annual return:

$$\text{Years to Double} \approx \frac{72}{\text{Annual Return Rate (\%) }}$$

*   At **$6\%$** annual return (FD / Debt): Doubling takes **$12$ years** ($72 / 6$).
*   At **$12\%$** annual return (Equity Mutual Funds / SIP): Doubling takes **$6$ years** ($72 / 12$).
*   At **$15\%$** annual return (Aggressive Small/Mid Cap): Doubling takes **$4.8$ years** ($72 / 15$).
