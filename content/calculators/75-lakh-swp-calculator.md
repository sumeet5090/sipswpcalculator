## The Mathematics of Capital Longevity: Withdrawal Rate vs. Compounding Velocity

The survival of your retirement capital depends on the balance between your **Monthly Withdrawal Rate ($W$)**, your **Portfolio Growth Rate ($r$)**, and your **Annual Inflation Hike ($s$)**:

$$ B_{m} = B_{m-1} \times (1 + r_m) - W_m $$

Where:
- **$B_m$** = Portfolio balance at the end of month $m$
- **$r_m$** = Monthly portfolio compounding return ($\text{Annual Return} \div 12$)
- **$W_m$** = Monthly cash payout withdrawn at month $m$

### The Golden Rule of Perpetual SWP
If your annual withdrawal rate is less than or equal to your portfolio's net compounding return ($W_{\text{annual}} \le r$), **your principal corpus never depletes**. At an 8% expected portfolio return on ₹75 Lakh:
- $8\% \times \text{₹75,00,000} = \text{₹6,00,000 / year} = \text{₹50,000 / month}$.
- Withdrawing **₹45,000 to ₹50,000 per month** allows you to withdraw cash indefinitely without eroding your original ₹75 Lakh principal!
