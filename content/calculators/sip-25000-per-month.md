## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many years will it take for ₹25,000 monthly SIP to reach ₹1 Crore?
At an expected 12% CAGR, a flat ₹25,000 monthly SIP crosses ₹1 Crore in **approximately 13.5 years** (on ₹40.5 Lakhs invested). With an annual 10% step-up, you reach ₹1 Crore in **just 11.2 years** (on ₹48.5 Lakhs invested).

### How is a ₹25,000 monthly SIP taxed under 2026 rules?
Mutual fund SIP installments are treated under **First-In, First-Out (FIFO)** rules. Each monthly installment held for longer than 12 months qualifies for Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) treatment under **Section 112A**. Cumulative gains up to ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year are 100% tax-free. Gains above ₹1.25 Lakh are taxed at **12.5% base rate (13.0% effective with 4% cess)**.

### Should I divide ₹25,000 across multiple mutual fund schemes?
Yes. Splitting ₹25,000 across 3 to 4 well-chosen funds (e.g. 1 Large-cap Index, 1 Flexi-cap, 1 Mid-cap, and 1 Small-cap) provides optimal asset diversification across market capitalizations while avoiding portfolio clutter.
